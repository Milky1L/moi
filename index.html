Read. Think. Write. Finish.

MOI, the Marketplace Of Ideas, is a writing app for people who read and think for a living or for love. One window takes an idea from source, to note, to finished paper.

One idea, four stages

Read

Mark up what you read. Highlights can carry a meaning, so a quote you want later is easy to find again.

Think

Link notes to each other with [[double brackets]]. Your ideas form a web you can walk through, not a pile you dig in.

Write

Draft in a calm editor with tables, pictures and maths. Notes are plain Markdown, so other apps can open them too.

Finish

Lay the paper out for handing in, and download it as Word or PDF. Google Docs can open the Word file.

What MOI promises

Your files stay yours

Notes are plain Markdown files in a folder you choose. If you stop using MOI, they are still just text.

No chatbot

MOI does not write for you and has no AI assistant. The thinking is yours.

Mac and Windows

The same app on both. This early version is for friends who will tell us what is confusing.

Made to be tested

It is unfinished on purpose. Try it and say what felt wrong.

    Install help

    MOI is not signed with a paid developer certificate yet, so your computer will warn you the first time. That is expected. Here is how to get past it.

    On a Mac

    1. Open the downloaded file and drag MOI into Applications.
    2. Open MOI. macOS will say it cannot verify it. Click Done.
    3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to MOI.
    4. Confirm. After that it opens normally.

    On Windows

    1. Run the downloaded installer.
    2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info.
    3. Click Run anyway.
    4. Follow the installer. After that it opens normally.

    Uninstall

    Mac: drag MOI from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings > Apps, find MOI, choose Uninstall.

    Your notes

    Uninstalling MOI does not delete your notes. They live in your own folder, as plain Markdown files, and stay there.
